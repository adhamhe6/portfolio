01 · About

Services that hold up in production

I'm a backend engineer with a Computer Science degree from Mansoura University and about three years of professional experience. Most of that time has gone into services that businesses rely on every day: inventory, purchasing, order management, point of sale and tax compliance.

I care about the less visible parts of the job: clean API contracts, sensible data models, fast queries and releases that are easy to roll back. I like working close to the people who use what I build, whether that's a warehouse team or a store manager, and turning their day-to-day problems into product decisions.

I also build AI features: LLM integrations, retrieval-augmented generation, semantic matching and forecasting. My team's AI data-analysis platform, Lotus, won the Cairo AI Hackathon 2025.
