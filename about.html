§ About

Engineer first, close to the people who use what I build.

01 · Background

The short version

I studied Computer Science at Mansoura University (2019–2023). I started with Django internships, and since 2024 I've worked full-time on platforms that businesses use every day: inventory, purchasing, production tracking, orders, point of sale and tax compliance.

Today I'm a backend engineer at Kulsys Technologies, working on Scalicx. It's an RFID-based ERP for garment and textile manufacturers, and my work turns raw tag reads from the factory floor into live stock and work-in-progress data. Before that, at myGINNE, I led the Egyptian Tax Authority e-receipt integration for 50+ retailers.

Alongside backend work I build AI features: LLM integrations, retrieval-augmented generation, semantic matching and forecasting. I completed the national DEPI Data Science & AI programme with a top-performer recognition, and my team's AI data-analysis platform, Lotus, won the Cairo AI Hackathon 2025.

02 · How I work

Principles I bring to a team

  1. 01

    Measure, then optimise

    Profile the slow path, fix the query or the index, and cache only where it pays off. That's how inventory reports went from 2.8s to 650ms.

  2. 02

    Boring releases

    Containerised deployments, small changes and quick rollbacks. Moving releases from 30 minutes to under 10 made shipping routine.

  3. 03

    Talk to the floor

    I work directly with production, warehouse and retail teams, and turn their real constraints into API and product decisions.

  4. 04

    AI where it earns its place

    Forecasting, semantic matching and LLM features built on a solid data layer, not bolted onto a shaky one.
